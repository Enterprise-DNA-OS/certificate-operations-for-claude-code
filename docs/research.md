## Claims boundary

DigiCert already provides dashboards, custom reports and natural-language inventory analysis. Do not claim it cannot answer these questions. This build's demonstrated difference is editable local records and rules linking service ownership, approval, recorded installation and current evidence. It does not duplicate certificate issuance, automated discovery, renewal protocols or deployment. Those are substantive incumbent capabilities, not merely a front end.
